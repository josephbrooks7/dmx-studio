# Character physique customization

Presets are editable starting points, not sex-dependent limits: slim, average, athletic, muscular, bodybuilder, curvy, full-bodied and stocky. Support independent muscle volume, muscle definition, body fat, breast/bust projection and underbust contour or pectoral/chest volume and definition, shoulder width, arm muscularity, upper/lower abdominal volume, abdominal definition, oblique contour, stomach fullness, waist width, hips, glute projection, thigh and calf volume. Both male and female characters can be athletic, muscular, soft, lean or larger-bodied. No automatic coupling of muscle volume to abdominal definition or body fat. Do not infer anatomy from wardrobe category. Refit garments to the actual morphed body while preserving body mesh, morph parameters, rig and garment design. Fit allowances distinguish tight from loose clothing; fabric should not artificially emphasize muscle definition. Validate varied combinations in front, side and three-quarter views, rest and posed, with unchanged body parameters across wardrobe changes. Current section-envelope fitting is experimental and does not yet implement detailed body morph targets or a production slider UI.
