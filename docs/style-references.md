# Style, pose and expression references (from Joseph's reference photos, Nov 28 2026)

Generic looks, poses and expressions learned from photos of performers. Characters never copy a real
person's face, likeness, name or signature branding; these are building blocks.

## Headwear looks (men and women)

| Look | Pieces | How it's worn |
|---|---|---|
| Wide-brim rock fedora | Black felt, flat wide brim, band of metal rings/conchos | Low, slightly forward; long hair below |
| Western rock hat | Cowboy hat (black or straw), decorated band, bandana under it | Brim curled at the sides, long straight hair down |
| Hip-hop fedora | Black felt fedora, pinched crown, short brim | Level, low on the brow; with gold rope chains and track jacket |
| Crooner trilby | Short-brim trilby with dark band | Tipped back and slightly to one side |
| Schoolboy cap | Soft wool cap with short peak (velvet/burgundy) | Low over the brow; hair out at the sides |
| Crochet tam | Big slouchy knit tam (stripes in red, yellow, green or white) | Back of the head, holds locs/long hair inside |
| Crochet bucket | Soft knitted bucket hat | Pulled down to the brows; braids out below |
| Top hat (glam) | Satin top hat, birdcage veil | Tipped forward and to one side — see "Like a Woman" |
| Baseball cap | Six-panel cap, curved bill | Forward or backwards; ponytail/bun through the back opening |

## Female artist styles ("Art Pop" family)

- **Art Pop Casual**: cropped tee (bright yellow or pink, small graphic), wide wrap trousers or a sarong
  over trousers, trainers.
- **Embroidered Red**: cropped red knit top, long A-line embroidered skirt, flat shoes; double buns.
- **Silk Wrap**: oversized metallic silk wrap/kimono-style coat with floral embroidery, worn closed.
- **Long Sleeves**: fitted patchwork rib knit with sleeves much longer than the arms, long sheer skirt;
  pigtails.
- **Raincoat Pop**: oversized yellow raincoat with dark collar, printed crop tee, black wide trousers,
  beaded bag; twisted mini buns.
- **Baby Pink**: pastel crop tee and shorts, heavy lace-up hiking boots; space buns.
- **Pink Lace Stage**: sheer beaded lace high-collar tunic over dark trousers; choppy bob with bangs,
  graphic winged liner, dark lip.
- **Simple Slip**: plain sleeveless shell top; long dark hair with bangs (singing at a mic stand).

Hairstyles needed: double space buns, mini twist buns, high pigtails, many small braids with beads/rings,
choppy bob with bangs, long hair with bangs.

## Poses (skeleton targets, all reachable within the joint-limit table)

| Pose | Description |
|---|---|
| T-stretch | Feet wide, arms straight out to the sides, palms down, head turned slightly |
| Hand on belly | Weight on one hip, one hand flat on the stomach, other at the waist; head tilted, chin down |
| Wrapped | Arms crossed inside a wrap, shoulders up, playful smile |
| Big reach | One arm straight up, the other out and down, body arched back, mouth wide open |
| Red-carpet shy | Feet together, shoulders forward, chin down, eyes up, slight smile |
| Chin on fists | Seated on a stool, elbows on knees, chin resting on both fists, knees together |
| Self-hug seated | Seated, one arm across the body holding the other arm, hand on knee |
| Lean-in seated | Seated, leaning far forward, hands clasped between knees, head tilted, hair falling |
| Mic in both hands | Both hands around the mic at the mouth, eyes closed, at a mic stand |
| Mic sweep | Mic held high to one side, the other forearm across the chest, looking down |
| Hands under chin | Fingertips meeting under the chin, elbows in, direct gaze |

Existing performance gestures (mic hold, peace sign, rock horns, arms up, crouch, wide stance) remain.
